# China pet beds: public-source evidence notes

Reviewed: 16 October 2026. Research methods example; not a current market-sizing, retail-price or sales-ranking report.

This note checks what the cited public pages can support. Provider estimates, historical coverage and directly observed facts are kept separate. Where the underlying method cannot be checked, a numerical claim is not treated as verified.

| Question | Public source and what it says | Evidence limitation | Usable conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can the two market-size figures be compared or ranked for reliability? | [SourceReady](https://www.sourceready.com/report/detail/china-pet-beds-market-report-2025) presents a shipments/export figure; [Deep Market Insights](https://deepmarketinsights.com/vista/insights/pet-beds-market/china) presents a China revenue estimate. | The accessible pages do not provide enough underlying records and matching scope definitions to audit these estimates or establish their comparability. We have not established that DMI measures domestic end-consumer expenditure. | Do not combine the estimates, classify them as independently verified export and domestic totals, or prefer one as a validated China-demand measure. Market size remains unverified in this example. |
| Is a mainstream retail price band established? | SourceReady and an [IndexBox report sales page](https://www.indexbox.io/store/china-kw-pet-bed-840-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/) describe price tiers. | Overlapping ranges do not establish a representative retail distribution or prove independent sourcing. The underlying listing sample, dates, product comparability and transaction mix have not been verified. | No current mainstream retail price band is established by this example. A usable product comparison requires identifiable listings, specifications, dates and price conditions. |
| Are the specific retail prices verified? | The original draft cites two Sina articles: [article A](https://k.sina.cn/article_7880068206_1d5b04c6e06809bd2k.html) and [article B](https://k.sina.cn/article_7879777046_1d5abdb1606801zfvm.html). | These pages could not be retrieved in this review. The article dates, product specifications and price conditions were not independently verified here. An access failure does not prove the articles or their prices are false. | Do not use the quoted amounts as current prices in this reviewed example. Recheck the original material before including any amount. |
| Does the social-search reference show current interest? | [Petdhw's republication](https://www.petdhw.com/jinhua-show-40731.html) is titled as an April 2024 Xiaohongshu pet-industry monthly report and is dated 22 May 2024. It describes cat/dog bed prominence within pet-supplies searches for that historical period. | It is a secondary republication of historical material. A current, corresponding first-party dashboard has not been checked. | It may be cited as dated historical context with attribution. It does not establish October 2026 search interest, sales, demand growth or willingness to pay. |

## Implication for a buyer

These references alone do not establish current China market size, a representative retail price distribution or winning products. For a current product comparison, agree the exact category and collect accessible product-level evidence first. Do not use this methods example as a substitute for that work.
