How three brands position sensitive-skin moisturisers in China

Question: which positioning themes are visible on selected manufacturer pages before we compare individual retail SKUs?

This example records published product details and manufacturer messaging. It does not verify clinical performance, current checkout prices, demand or sales rankings.

Curél · 珂润润浸保湿滋润乳霜

Published detail: Kao China's product page identifies a 40 g cream. Its China product-family page also lists a 70 g format.

Manufacturer messaging: ceramide-function ingredient, moisturising care and a light feel.

Not verified: current retail price, promotions, stock and transaction data. The two pack sizes must not be treated as the same retail SKU.

Sources: Kao China product page · China product-family page. Publication dates not stated on the reviewed pages.

WINONA · 薇诺娜舒敏保湿特护霜第二代

Published detail: the official solutions page explicitly names a second-generation cream.

Manufacturer messaging: the cream appears within a staged sensitivity-care routine alongside other products.

Not verified: pack size, checkout price and availability for this exact generation. A separate search result for a 50 g listing was not independently retrieved and is not used as a current offer.

Source: WINONA solutions page. Publication date not stated on the reviewed page.

Dr.Yu · 玉泽专研青蒿修护面霜

Published detail: a manufacturer article dated 25 August 2026 describes separate dry-sensitive and oily-sensitive cream variants.

Manufacturer messaging: artemisia-led barrier-care positioning and segmentation by skin type.

Not verified: exact retail pack sizes, prices and sales. Brand claims and technical explanations in the article have not been independently validated here.

Source: Shanghai Jahwa article about Dr.Yu. Published 25 August 2026.

What this means for a product team

Interpretation: sensitivity and barrier-care language is already present in these selected examples. A proposed position based only on those broad terms would need a clearer point of difference. This is an inference from three examples, not a finding about market-wide demand.

Before comparing price or choosing a launch SKU, match the exact product generation, variant and pack size, then verify an accessible retail offer with its date and promotion conditions. Those checks are not completed by this example.

How to read the evidence

“Checked” is our review date, not a source's publication date. An official page is primary evidence of what a manufacturer publishes; it is not independent proof that its claims or rankings are true.
